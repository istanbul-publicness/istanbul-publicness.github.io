Ground publicness of Istanbul

Every square metre of each 500 m cell is assigned an access regime (Dovey & Pafka 2020) from OpenStreetMap geometry; Foursquare and Overture places decide what a building's ground floor is. The cell score is the area-weighted mean of the regime scores.

Method and data

Scores: open-public 1, open-private 0.50, quasi-public 0.47, ticketed 0.38, invitation 0.13, inaccessible 0 (P = A × [0.5 + 0.5 C]). Residential yards count as invitation; urban groves under 50 ha and grass as open-public; the first 30 m from the sea shore, where OpenStreetMap leaves it unmapped, as open-public under the Coastal Law (Bosphorus excluded). Car space, larger forests and farmland are held out of the score and reported as shares. Uncertainty: unknown ground counted as 0 or 1 (missing-data range) and five alternative rule sets (rule range); grade A when both are narrow.
